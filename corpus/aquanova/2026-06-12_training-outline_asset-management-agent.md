## 1. Goal

Planners can use the agent for their daily questions, know what it can and cannot do in this first pilot phase, and know how to give feedback. One session of 90 minutes per group of seven.

## 2. What the pilot offers on day one

- Questions about **maintenance procedures**, **technical sheets** and, from this week, **inspection reports** (except about 1,400 older scanned reports).
- Answers with sources and document dates.
- Not yet: questions about a specific asset's history or location. The tools on the asset register are in development and will be added during the pilot. Until then, the agent says it cannot look up asset records.

We start with documents only on purpose. Planners told us in March that one wrong asset answer would cost more trust than a missing feature.

## 3. Programme

| Time | Block |
|---|---|

| 09:40 | Demo: three real questions from the evaluation set |
| 09:55 | How the agent finds answers: knowledge base, sources, "I don't know" |
| 10:10 | Hands-on: each planner asks five of their own questions |

| 10:50 | What's next: tools, timeline, the next evaluation |

## 4. Key messages

1. **Always check the source** for anything you will act on. The agent shows the document and its date. Click it.
2. **"I don't know" is a good answer.** If the agent says it found nothing, it has not guessed.
3. **You only see what you are allowed to see.** The agent respects the same document permissions as the libraries.
4. **Your feedback trains the evaluation, not the model.** Thumbs down and a comment go into the evaluation set; nobody retrains a model on your chats.
5. Do not paste personal data of customers or colleagues into questions.

## 5. Privacy and logging

- Conversations are logged in AquaNova's tenant for 90 days, for quality review by the project team and the pilot coordinator.
- Logs are not used to assess individual planners. This was agreed with Kevin Hermans and AquaNova's privacy officer.

## 6. Hands-on exercise

Each planner gets a sheet with:

- two questions from the evaluation set with known answers,
- three empty lines for their own questions,
- a column to note "useful / not useful / wrong".

The sheets are collected and added to the evaluation set where useful.

## 7. Practical

- Access through the group "AMA pilot"; Kevin has checked all 14 accounts.
- Web chat link on the asset management intranet page.
- Support: the pilot coordinator first, then Pieter (Wednesdays on site).
- Summer: most planners take leave in July or August. We expect lower use then, and plan the first real review in September.

## 8. Materials

- Slides (12) with the demo questions
- One-page "how to ask good questions" (be specific, name the asset type, ask follow-ups)
- FAQ, maintained by the pilot coordinator
