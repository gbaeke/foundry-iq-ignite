A few changes in the team that I want you to hear from me directly, before they go around the coffee machine.

In her new role she will be responsible for AI engineering practices across projects, coach the more junior colleagues and work closely with Pieter on architecture. Congratulations, Amira, well deserved.

What this means for you in practice:

If you have concerns or questions, my door is open, or just send me a message on Teams.

Lotte
