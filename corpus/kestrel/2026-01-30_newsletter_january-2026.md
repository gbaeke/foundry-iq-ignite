## Started: Clinical Knowledge Assistant

The **Clinical Knowledge Assistant** at Mediva Care Group kicked off on **2026-01-12**. Dr. Hilde Van Damme, Chief Medical Information Officer, is sponsor. Pieter, Amira and Ruben ran the first workshops with clinicians and with Mediva IT. Two early conclusions: the guideline library is bigger and messier than expected, and the clinicians are very willing to help build an evaluation set, as long as we keep the sessions short. Sarah Mertens, Mediva's DPO, reviewed the data flows in the second week, which by now is simply how we start.

## Starting Monday: Asset Management Agent

On **2026-02-02** we start the **Asset Management Agent** at AquaNova, the water utility in Leuven, with Filip Wuyts, Director Asset Management, as sponsor. The agent helps asset planners find information on assets, maintenance history and inspections, combining documents with data from AquaNova's systems. It is built on Microsoft Foundry, with Foundry Agent Service for the agent and its tools and Foundry IQ for the knowledge base. Pieter is lead architect, Jonas handles the platform and Ruben builds the agent tools. On the AquaNova side, Kevin Hermans is the IT architect; some of you know him from his time at Fortalis.

Ruben is now on two engagements at the same time. Pieter and I keep an eye on his load; if you see him working at 22:00, tell me.
