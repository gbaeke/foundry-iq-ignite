Hi all,

The first quarter is behind us, and a busy one. A few highlights, as usual kept short. The longer stories are for the coffee machine.

## Q1 in numbers (roughly)

- Utilisation for the quarter ended just under 70%, slightly below our target, mainly because of a slow January. February and March were full.
- Timesheets: thank you to everyone who closed March before today. The finance team can invoice on Monday instead of chasing on Wednesday. Please keep it up.

## Certifications

Three colleagues passed an Azure certification this quarter, and two more are scheduled for April. Reminder: the training budget is EUR 2,500 per person per year, and exam fees for the priority certifications are paid on top of that if you pass the first time. Book your exam through the finance team so the invoice goes to Kestrel directly.

## Knowledge sharing ("kennisdeling")

We restart the internal knowledge sharing sessions in April. Format: one hour, Friday afternoon, two short talks and questions, followed by a drink for those in the office. Elien coordinates the agenda. If you have learned something on a project that others could reuse (a deployment trick, a customer workshop format, a mistake you would not make again), send her a title and two lines. Customer names are fine in the room, but slides that leave the room are anonymised.

## Office

- The second meeting room finally has a working screen. The cable drawer has been labelled; please put adapters back.
- Bike shed: the new rack is installed. Parking in the courtyard stays reserved for visitors and the delivery van of the bakery next door.
- The office will be closed on Easter Monday, 10 April.

## Pipeline

Several proposals are out, mostly in healthcare and logistics. I will share more once they are signed; for now, keep your calendars a bit flexible from May onwards. If you are asked to help with a proposal, please give it priority, even if it is only an hour of estimating.

## Spring team day

Save the date: Friday 12 May, a full day out of the office. The programme is a mix of a short strategy session in the morning and something outdoors in the afternoon (no, not paintball again). Partners and kids are welcome for the barbecue at the end.

## A small request

Our website still shows three people who left last year and none of the people who joined. If you have not sent a recent photo and a two-line bio to Elien, please do so before mid-April.

Have a good Easter break, those who take it.

Lotte
