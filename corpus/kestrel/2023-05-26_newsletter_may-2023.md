## New engagement: Bed Capacity Dashboard at Mediva

On 2023-05-08 we kicked off the **Bed Capacity Dashboard** for **Mediva Care Group**, the hospital group in Ghent. The sponsor is Jeroen Lambrecht, their IT Manager. The goal is simple to explain and harder to do: replace the morning phone round, in which bed managers call every ward for occupancy figures, with one shared view of beds across the three campuses.

The team is small. I am engagement lead and project manager myself, Sofie Claes is building the data layer on Azure Synapse Analytics, and Elien Jacobs designs the Power BI reports. It is a fixed-price engagement with a clear scope, which is exactly why we kept the team compact.

First impressions from the kickoff: the hospital information system is fine, the master data on beds is not. Agreeing which beds "exist" (and which are blocked for isolation or maintenance) will take more conversations than the pipelines. Sofie has started a bed list per ward with the bed management office.

It is our first engagement at Mediva, so a word of caution for anyone visiting the campuses with us: badges are mandatory, laptops never on a nursing station desk, and no photos inside the wards, not even of a whiteboard.
