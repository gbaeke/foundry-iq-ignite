## Knowledge graph experiment: first results

In the tech radar, Pieter put knowledge graphs for retrieval in the **Trial** ring, starting with an internal experiment on our own project documents. Pieter and Ruben have now run the first comparison. They indexed a copy of our internal documents (closing reports, status reports, kennisdeling notes) and asked the same fixed set of questions three ways: plain retrieval, an entity graph and a topic graph.

Early impressions, not conclusions:
- For "what does document X say" questions, plain retrieval is as good and cheaper.
- For "who worked with whom" and "which engagements had a similar problem" questions, the graphs do noticeably better, as long as people and customers are resolved correctly. Similar names are a real trap.
- For broad "what are the recurring themes" questions, the topic graph gives the most useful overview.

The full write-up follows in Q4, when we decide whether this moves to Adopt. A reminder that the experiment uses our internal documents only; no customer data was copied.
