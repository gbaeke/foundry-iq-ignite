## New engagement: Scaldis Ops Copilot

On **2025-01-20** we kicked off the **Scaldis Ops Copilot** at Scaldis Logistics in Antwerp. Koen Verstraete, the CIO, is sponsor and Nathalie Dumont, Head of Terminal Operations, is product owner. The idea: shift leaders and planners ask questions about the terminal's SOPs and work instructions in Microsoft Teams and get an answer with a link to the source procedure in SharePoint. It is built on Azure OpenAI and Azure AI Search.

Pieter De Smet is lead architect, Thomas Maes project manager, Elien Jacobs takes UX and front end, and Ruben builds the Teams bot. Target go-live is **2025-07-01**. It is a time-and-materials engagement, so please keep your timesheets precise and your descriptions meaningful; Thomas reports burn to the customer every two weeks.

## Closed: Line Sensor Data Platform
