## Quality Inspection Vision: the light on line 3

At Verhaeghe Foods, the **Quality Inspection Vision** pilot on line 3 ran through the summer. The camera model flagged far too many good products as defective at certain hours of the day. Amira traced it to inconsistent lighting: daylight from the skylights above the line, plus reflections on the stainless steel frame. Jonas is now testing a closed camera enclosure with its own LED lighting at the inspection point. The first days look promising. Ruben's labelling tool keeps the quality team busy in the meantime: the target of 4,000 labelled images is in sight.

A lesson for every vision project, which Amira asked me to repeat here: walk the line at different hours of the day before you choose a camera position.
