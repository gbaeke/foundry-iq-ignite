Welcome to Kestrel. This guide explains how your first weeks will go and where to find things. It was updated ahead of our next new colleague, **Ruben Wouters**, who joins on **2025-01-06** as Junior Developer. If something in here is wrong or outdated, tell me; that is the fastest way to fix it.

## Before day one

- Your laptop is prepared by Jonas Peeters and ready a few days before you start. You pick it up on day one; accounts are active from 08:00 that morning.
- You receive an invitation for the Monday breakfast and the name of your **onboarding buddy**. The buddy is your first point of contact for every question, including the ones you think are too small to ask.

## Week 1: getting settled

| Day | What |
|---|---|
| Monday | Breakfast with whoever is in the office, laptop and accounts, office tour, lunch with Lotte |
| Tuesday | Security and data handling session (GDPR policy note, acceptable use, MFA on all accounts) |
| Wednesday | How we work: project template, first-week checklist, timesheets, expenses |
| Thursday | Our engineering guidelines: Bicep conventions, AI evaluation practices, code review |
| Friday | Shadow a customer meeting or a sprint review, with your buddy |

## Week 2: internal training

- Hands-on with our infrastructure templates in a sandbox subscription: deploy, break, redeploy.
- Walk-through of the Teams app and web front-end starter templates.
- Read two closing reports of past engagements, chosen by your buddy, and discuss them. Closing reports are the best summary of how we actually work.

From week 3, most new colleagues join a customer engagement. Your first assignment is agreed with Lotte before you start.

## Tools and where things are

- **Microsoft Teams** for chat, calls and project channels. One channel per engagement; customer-facing conversations stay in the customer's own environment where they ask for it.
- **SharePoint** for documents: project folders, templates, kennisdeling slides, policies.
- **Resource planner**: updated every Monday by Lotte. Check your allocation for the coming two weeks.
- **Timesheets**: weekly, before Friday 17:00. At month-end, before the last working day 12:00.

## How we work

- **Two office days per week**, flexible, plus all-hands and kennisdeling Fridays.
- **Customer first, but not customer only.** Internal work (templates, talks, proposals) is part of the job and has its own codes.
- **Ask early.** If you are stuck for more than half a day, ask. Nobody here earns points for suffering alone.
- **Write it down.** Decisions, assumptions and open questions go into the project channel or the status report, not only into a call.

## Security basics

- MFA on every account, no exceptions.
- Customer data stays in customer environments. Never on personal devices or consumer AI tools.
- Lock your screen when you walk away, also in the office. The penalty is croissants for the team.

## Learning

- Training budget: EUR 2,500 per person per year.
- Priority certifications: Azure AI Engineer, Databricks Data Engineer Associate.
- Two kennisdeling sessions per half year. Presenting at one in your first year is encouraged, not mandatory.

## Your first 90 days

At the end of month one and month three you have a short check-in with Lotte and your buddy: what went well, what is unclear, what you want to learn next. There is no formal evaluation in this period; it is a conversation.
