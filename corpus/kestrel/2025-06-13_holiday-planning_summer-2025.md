Hi all,

The summer is coming faster than the planning. Here is what you need to know.

## Key dates

- **Monday 21 July**: national holiday, office closed. Many of you will take the Friday before as a bridge day; please check with your project team first.
- **Friday 15 August**: Assumption Day, office closed.
- **Bouwverlof**: from 21 July to 8 August. Customers in construction and several manufacturing plants work with reduced crews or stop completely. No site visits, installations or network changes at those customers in that period, even if the plan says otherwise.
- **Kick-off of the new season**: Monday 1 September, breakfast in the office.

## Rules (unchanged)

1. At least one person reachable per running engagement, every week of the summer.
2. Maximum three consecutive weeks without prior agreement.
3. Tell your customer contact four weeks in advance, and put your absence and your backup in the status report.
4. Out-of-office message in English and Dutch, with the name of your backup.
5. No production deployments in the last two days before your holiday.

## Specific points this year

**Thomas at 60%.** As announced in the staffing plan, Thomas Maes works 60% from **2025-07-01**. His fixed days are Monday, Tuesday and Thursday. Project steering committees he chairs should be planned on those days. On the other days, questions about planning go to me.

**Customer go-lives in or right after the summer.** If your engagement has a go-live in September, the weeks before are not the time for a three-week holiday of the whole team. Talk to me now, not in August. Also plan the hypercare weeks with the customer: who is available, how users report issues, who decides on a rollback.

**Customer freezes.** Some customers do not allow changes in the summer peak, or require extra approvals for anything touching production. Ports and terminals in particular have peak volumes in July and August. Check and add it to your plan.

## Holiday calendar

Add your weeks to the shared calendar in Teams **before 27 June**. I will go through every engagement in the first week of July. Overlaps within one engagement are solved together.

## If you have open days

- Update the project template with the "day zero checklist" coming out of the June kennisdeling session
- Certification preparation (the 2025 budget is still less than half used)
- Clean up sandbox resources (Jonas will send the monthly cost list anyway)

## Summer drink

Thursday 3 July, from 17:00, in the courtyard. Families welcome.

Enjoy the summer, and switch off for real.
