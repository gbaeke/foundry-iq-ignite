Hi all,

Happy new year once more, for those I have not seen yet. A few highlights from January.

## Looking back on 2023

The final numbers are in. 2023 was our best year so far in revenue, with a utilisation of 71% across the year. More important to me: every engagement that closed in 2023 was accepted by the customer without open issues. Thank you all.

## Engagements

**Fortalis Landing Zone** enters its last month. Jonas Peeters is wrapping up the hub-spoke landing zone at Fortalis Verzekeringen in Brussels, with all governance rules deployed as policy-as-code. Handover sessions with the customer's architecture team are planned for mid-February, and the engagement is on track to close on time. Jonas will share the reusable parts of the policy set in the template repository afterwards, minus anything customer-specific.

Other engagements are running as planned; details as usual in the weekly planning meeting.

## Hiring update

- A **cloud engineer** joins us in March. Welcome lunch and onboarding details will follow closer to the date.
- The **data engineering vacancy** reopens this month. We are looking for someone with at least three years of experience in pipelines and data modelling on Azure; Databricks experience is a plus.
- We also had more spontaneous applications for junior roles than ever. We will not open a junior position before the end of the year, but I keep the good ones on file.

As always: a referral from you is worth more than any recruitment agency. Introduce candidates to me directly.

## Certifications and training

- 2024 training budget: EUR 2,500 per person, unchanged. The priority certifications for this year are Azure AI Engineer and Databricks Data Engineer Associate.
- New this year: you can use up to two days of working time for exam preparation, to be logged on the internal training code.

## Knowledge sharing

Elien is planning two kennisdeling sessions in the first half of the year. The first one in March; proposed topics so far are working with operational event data and our new Bicep conventions. If you have not read Jonas' guideline yet, now is the moment.

## Office and practical

- New year reception: thank you to everyone who came to the dinner on the 12th. The quiz was won, again, by the data team. A rematch has been requested.
- The heating in the big room has been fixed. Mostly.
- From February, the Friday lunch is back: sandwiches on the first Friday of the month, ordered by whoever is in the office first.

## Reminder on data handling

A reminder that customer data never goes onto personal devices or personal cloud storage, and test extracts are deleted from laptops at the end of an engagement. An updated policy note on handling customer data under GDPR will follow in spring.
