Hi all,

Time for the yearly summer puzzle. Below are the rules we agreed at the partner meeting, the key dates, and what I need from you.

## Key dates

- **Friday 21 July**: national holiday, office closed.
- **Tuesday 15 August**: Assumption Day, office closed.
- **Weeks 30 to 33** (24 July to 18 August): the busiest holiday weeks. Expect reduced capacity at Kestrel and at most customers.
- Several of our industrial and construction-related contacts follow the **bouwverlof** (this year roughly the last week of July and the first two weeks of August). If your customer works with contractors or has plant shutdowns, check with them now which weeks their people and their sites are unavailable. Installations, site visits and network changes do not happen in those weeks, whatever the plan says.

## Rules

1. **Coverage per engagement.** Every running engagement keeps at least one person reachable who knows the solution, in every week of the summer. If you are the only person on a project, agree a backup with me before you book.
2. **Maximum three consecutive weeks** without prior agreement. Longer is possible, but talk to me first.
3. **Customer communication.** Tell your customer contact at least four weeks in advance when you are away, and who replaces you. Put it in the status report as well, not only in an email.
4. **Out-of-office message** in English and Dutch, with the name of your backup. No phone numbers of colleagues in the message, please; use the general office number.
5. **No deployments to production** in the last two days before you leave. Yes, also on "small" changes.

## Planning per engagement

Please add your weeks to the shared holiday calendar in Teams **before 7 July**. I will then go over each engagement with the people on it. Where two people on the same engagement overlap, we will look for a solution together; first come, first served is not the rule, but it does help.

For the dashboard work at Mediva, the hospital itself runs on reduced staff in July and August, and the bed management office has asked us not to schedule user sessions between 17 July and 15 August. Sofie, Elien and I will plan the build work for those weeks and keep the usability rounds for September.

## Bench and internal work

If you have open days in the summer, there is plenty of internal work:
- the reusable infrastructure templates (talk to Jonas)
- the project template and first-week checklist
- certification preparation; the training budget can be used for this

Please log internal work on the internal project codes so we can see what was done.

## After the summer

Kick-off of the new season is on Friday 1 September, with breakfast in the office. Enjoy the summer, rest well, and leave the laptop at home.

Lotte
