Hi all,

Practical information for the end of the year. Please read it to the end; the last section is about timesheets, and the finance team will thank you.

## Office closure

- The office is **closed from Monday 25 December to Monday 1 January** inclusive.
- Tuesday 26 December is not a public holiday in Belgium, but we close anyway. These days are not taken from your holiday balance; they are a gift from the partners, as every year.
- We reopen on **Tuesday 2 January 2024**. New year breakfast at 9:00.

## Change freeze

From **Friday 22 December 12:00 until Wednesday 3 January** no production changes on customer environments, unless the customer explicitly asks for it and the change is approved by the engagement lead. This includes infrastructure, data pipelines and model deployments.

Several customers run their own year-end freeze, often longer than ours. Container terminals and hospitals are especially strict around the holidays, and industrial customers often use the shutdown weeks for maintenance, which means network changes and site visits are off the table. Check your customer's freeze dates and put them in your project plan now. A freeze that surprises the plan in week 51 is a planning mistake, not bad luck.

## On-call

We do not run formal on-call for customers. For engagements where Kestrel supports a production system during hypercare, the engagement lead names one person reachable by phone during working hours on 27 to 29 December. Please make sure the customer has that name and the office number.

## Before you leave

1. Hand over open work: write a short status in the project channel, with what is waiting on whom.
2. Set your out-of-office, in English and Dutch, with a backup name.
3. Shut down or scale down development compute you started. Idle clusters over the holidays are a classic. Jonas will run a cost check on our internal subscriptions on 21 December and will send friendly but public reminders.
4. Lock your laptop in the cupboard if you leave it in the office.

## Year-end drink

The all-hands and year-end drink is on **Thursday 21 December from 16:00**. Short look back on the year, a look at January, then food. Partners welcome from 18:00.

## Timesheets and invoicing

- All December hours must be in by **Friday 22 December, 12:00**. The days after Christmas can be filled in on 2 January.
- Fixed-price engagements: please also update the remaining effort estimate. We close the books per engagement at year-end.
- Expense claims for 2023 must be submitted before 5 January.

Thank you all for a strong year. Rest well, and see you in January.

Lotte
