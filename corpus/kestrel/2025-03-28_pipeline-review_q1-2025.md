## 1. Summary

Q1 closed with two signatures and a healthy, but concentrated, pipeline. Most of our revenue in 2025 comes from five existing customers. That is good for relationships and risky for planning: two budget decisions at the same customer can move a quarter. New logos remain a priority for the second half, without chasing work outside our Microsoft data and AI focus.

## 2. Signed this quarter

| Engagement | Customer | Type | Value | Start |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quality Inspection Vision | Verhaeghe Foods | Fixed price | EUR 110,000 | 2025-03-10 |
| Broker Portal Modernisation, discovery | Fortalis Verzekeringen | Time and materials | EUR 30,000 | 2025-04-14 |

**Quality Inspection Vision** builds on the good relationship with Dries Callens and Ann Verhaeghe. Amira El Idrissi leads it. It started on time.

**Broker Portal discovery** is a short discovery phase for a new broker portal at Fortalis, with Laura Vermeulen as product owner and Marc Dubois as sponsor. Elien Jacobs will be UX lead and Thomas Maes project manager. The discovery has no technology choice yet; the outcome is a roadmap and an estimate for a build phase. This is the most uncertain item on the list: a build phase would be large, but Fortalis is also preparing for new regulatory requirements, and budgets there are under pressure.

## 3. Accounts

**Scaldis Logistics.** Strong relationship with Koen Verstraete and Nathalie Dumont. Koen mentioned interest in further use cases in terminal operations, but nothing concrete before the autumn. Action: keep it warm, no proposal yet.

**Mediva Care Group.** Dr. Hilde Van Damme remains interested in further work with us. Mediva's budget round closes in the autumn; realistic start of anything new is 2026. Jeroen Lambrecht asked for a general check-in in May. Action: Lotte to schedule.

**Fortalis Verzekeringen.** See above. Marc Dubois is pragmatic and fair, but his budget is under pressure from regulation. Action: Lotte to have a coffee with Marc in May, independent of the discovery.

**Verhaeghe Foods.** Very positive. Ann Verhaeghe values that we spoke the language of the plant floor. Action: none before the summer; let the current work speak.

**AquaNova.** Good collaboration with Filip Wuyts and his team. Filip has hinted at interest in further AI use cases, but budgets are set per year and next year's are not known yet. Action: Lotte to propose a short inspiration session in September.

## 5. Risks

- **Concentration**: the top two customers represent more than half of planned 2025 revenue.
- **Regulatory budgets**: insurance and banking customers are redirecting budget to compliance programmes.
- **Capacity in H2**: Thomas moves to 60% from July, which limits how many engagements we can run with a dedicated project manager.
