## 1. Welcome back

Lotte opened with a short look back on the summer: no incidents on any running engagement during the holiday weeks, and the backup arrangements worked. Two customers explicitly thanked us for the advance notice of absences. We will keep the same rules next year.

## 2. Engagements

**Container Dwell-Time Forecasting (Scaldis Logistics).** Kicked off on 2023-09-04. Scaldis, the container terminal operator in Antwerp, wants a forecast of how long import containers will stay on the yard, so the yard planners can stack them smarter. Sponsor is Koen Verstraete, the CIO; Nathalie Dumont, Head of Terminal Operations, is the product owner. Thomas Maes is project manager, with Sofie Claes on data engineering and Amira El Idrissi on the forecasting model. Bram Visser of DataForge joins as subcontractor for the Databricks part. Thomas: "The first two weeks were mostly about understanding gate-in and gate-out events. The terminal has its own vocabulary, and we are learning it."

**Bed Capacity Dashboard (Mediva Care Group).** The first campus went live this month. Bed managers no longer do the full phone round there. The other two campuses follow in October once the ward mapping is validated. Elien showed a few screens; the drill-down per ward got spontaneous applause from the people who had seen the old spreadsheet.

## 3. Capacity and planning

- Utilisation in Q3 so far is good, around 74%.
- Sofie is on two engagements at the same time until the dashboard closes at the end of November. Please do not plan internal work for her before December.
- Bench for October is low. If you are asked to support a proposal, please make time.

## 4. Internal initiatives

- **Infrastructure templates.** Jonas presented a first version of our Bicep modules for networking, identity and monitoring. A short guideline on Bicep conventions will follow in November. Feedback welcome in the Teams channel.
- **Project template.** The first-week checklist is drafted. It now asks every project to name the customer's data owner and IT security contact in week one.
- **Knowledge sharing.** The autumn session is planned for early December. Topic ideas so far: dashboards that people actually use, and working with operational event data.
