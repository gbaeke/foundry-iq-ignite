## 2. Engagements

**Quality Inspection Vision (Verhaeghe Foods)** closed on **2025-11-14**, on time and within the fixed price. The model reaches **96% defect recall** on the final test set, on **4,000 labelled images**. The false positives on line 3 disappeared once the camera enclosure was installed, and the same enclosure was used on the other lines from day one. Amira presented the results to Ann Verhaeghe and Dries Callens, who signed off without remarks. The enclosure design is now part of our standard edge vision setup.

**Clinical Knowledge Assistant (Mediva Care Group)** was signed this week and starts on **2026-01-12**. Physicians and nurses will be able to ask questions about Mediva's own clinical guidelines and protocols and get answers with references to the source. The team: Pieter De Smet as lead architect, Amira as lead AI engineer, Ruben Wouters as developer. It is built on Microsoft Foundry, with a Foundry IQ knowledge base and Azure OpenAI. Pieter: "The hard part will not be the model. It will be knowing which guideline is the current one."

Other engagements run as planned and are covered in the weekly planning meeting.
