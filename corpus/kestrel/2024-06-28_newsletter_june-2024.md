## Two engagements started this quarter

**Claims Triage Automation (Fortalis Verzekeringen).** Started on 2024-04-08. Fortalis, the property and casualty insurer in Brussels, wants incoming claims to be read, classified and routed automatically, so claims handlers spend their time on the difficult files. Laura Vermeulen, Claims Operations Manager, is the product owner; Marc Dubois, Chief Digital Officer, is the sponsor. Thomas Maes is project manager, Jonas Peeters handles the platform and Amira El Idrissi the models. First milestone: a labelled set of claims for the main property claim types, agreed with Laura's team.

**Line Sensor Data Platform (Verhaeghe Foods).** Kicked off on 2024-06-03 in Roeselare. Verhaeghe produces frozen vegetables and wants sensor data from its production lines in one place, so quality can trace temperatures per batch instead of searching paper logs. Ann Verhaeghe, the CEO, sponsors it, and Dries Callens, Quality Manager, is product owner. Sofie Claes leads the engagement, with Jonas on IoT and platform and Bram Visser of DataForge on the Databricks jobs. Sofie's first message from the plant: "It is cold, it is loud, and the sensors are older than me."

Both engagements are fixed price. A reminder to all project teams: every new request from the customer goes on the parking lot list first and gets an estimate before anyone starts working on it.
