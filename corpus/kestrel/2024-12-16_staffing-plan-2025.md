## 1. Purpose

This plan sets out the main staffing changes and capacity assumptions for 2025. It is not a full allocation sheet; the detailed weekly planning stays in the resource planner and is updated every Monday. The focus here is on changes in the team and on the first half of the year, where we have the most certainty.
