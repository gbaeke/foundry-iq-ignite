Hi Jeroen,

We looked at it with Amira. Three causes:

1. **Regenerations.** In the first two weeks of the pilot, physicians regenerated the draft on average 1.6 times per letter, mostly to "see if it gets better". Each regeneration is a full two-step call. In the evaluation this was 1.1.
2. **Evaluation tail.** The September invoice still includes the last part of the test runs on historical stays we did for the certainty wording change at the end of August.
3. **Long stays.** Geriatrics has more long stays in the pilot than in our estimate sample, and long stays use the condensation step.

Per signed letter we are at roughly 1.3 times the estimate. Still well within what Dr. Van Damme considered reasonable in the steering committee.

What we propose:
- Regenerate only the paragraph a physician is unhappy with, instead of the whole draft. Amira can do that before handover.
- Show a short note in the review screen that regenerating rarely changes much.
- Switch off the test deployment after handover.

For 2025, budget the monthly figure of the pilot times twelve, plus 30% margin if other departments are added. I will put it in the consumption report of early October.

Pieter
