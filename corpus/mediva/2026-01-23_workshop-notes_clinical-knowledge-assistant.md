## 1. Where the sources are

| Source | Content | Approximate size |
|---|---|---|
| Intranet document library | Current guidelines, protocols, procedures per department | about 2,900 documents |
| Legacy document share | Older protocols, local working instructions, some still in use | about 4,600 files |
| Nursing procedures handbook | Exported pages, updated by the nursing directorate | about 380 procedures |

Formats: mostly text documents and PDF, some scanned PDFs, a few spreadsheets with dosing tables, and slide decks from training sessions.

## 2. Metadata today

The intranet library has fields for department, owner, version and review date, but they are filled in inconsistently:

- Owner field empty for roughly one third of the documents
- Review date in the past for many documents that are still used
- Version sometimes in the field, sometimes in the file name ("v3_final_def"), sometimes only on the cover page

The legacy share has no metadata at all beyond folder names and file dates.

The quality officer explained that accreditation requires every protocol to have an owner and a review date, but enforcement has focused on the intranet library. The legacy share was supposed to be phased out in 2023.

## 3. Department selection

Criteria agreed with Hilde: many protocols, many questions outside office hours, a guideline coordinator who can spend time on content, and a manageable number of documents.

| Department | Docs (intranet) | Night/weekend questions | Coordinator available | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cardiology | about 210 | Medium | Yes | High |
| Emergency | about 340 | High | Yes | High |
| Intensive care | about 260 | High | Limited until April | Medium |
| Oncology | about 420 | Low | Yes | Medium |

**Proposal to the steering committee of 2026-01-28: cardiology and emergency.** Hilde supports this. Intensive care is a candidate for a later phase.

## 4. Scope of sources for the first knowledge base

- Phase 1 starts with the intranet library documents of cardiology and emergency.
- Documents in the legacy share are included only if a coordinator confirms they are in use and not available in the intranet library.
- Scanned PDFs: included when text can be extracted; otherwise listed for the coordinators.
- Training slide decks: excluded; they often simplify the protocol.
- Nursing procedures: emergency nursing procedures included, as night questions often come from nurses.

## 5. Version questions

The coordinators admitted that the same protocol may exist in several places, for instance a current version on the intranet and an older copy on the legacy share or in another department's folder. The SOW assumes that Mediva nominates content owners who confirm which version is current. Both coordinators agreed to do that for their departments; how much effort this takes is not clear yet. We note it as a risk for the steering committee.
