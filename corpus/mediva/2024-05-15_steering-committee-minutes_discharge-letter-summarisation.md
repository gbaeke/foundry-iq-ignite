## Present

Dr. Hilde Van Damme (chair, CMIO), Jeroen Lambrecht (IT Manager), Sarah Mertens (DPO), Pieter De Smet, Amira El Idrissi, Thomas Maes. Lotte Willems excused.

## 1. Status

Thomas presented the status: foundation phase closed on 2024-04-12, prototype phase in sprint P2, overall green. The fixed price of EUR 120,000 is not under pressure; the first invoice of 30% has been paid.

## 2. Demonstration

Amira showed three drafts on historical stays (pseudonymised): one short internal medicine stay, one multi-problem stay and one long geriatric stay. Pieter explained the two-step approach and the first measurements: draft time down to about 24 seconds, statements without a supporting source down from 7.1% to 1.9%.

Dr. Van Damme's reaction: the medication section is "exactly what we hoped for". She was less convinced by the course of stay on the geriatric example, which she found too long and too descriptive. Amira will tighten the template.

## 3. Privacy

Sarah confirmed that no new questions have come up since the check-in of April. She asked that the repeat pseudonymisation test is done before any physician sees drafts of recent stays in the evaluation. Agreed.

## 4. Evaluation plan

- Dr. Van Damme has received **12 names** of physicians, six per department, mixing assistants and staff members.
- Thomas proposed to start with a baseline period in which physicians write letters as usual while their time is measured, followed by the period with drafts. Dr. Van Damme agreed; measuring time is better than asking for an opinion afterwards.
- Evaluation start is planned on 2024-06-24, after a training session in the week of 17 June. Amira will write the evaluation protocol.
- Risk: summer holidays. Of the 12, four have leave in the second half of July. The protocol must allow physicians to skip weeks.

## 5. Costs

Pieter presented the consumption estimate: well under one euro per letter, a low four-digit amount per year for the two pilot departments. Jeroen asked for monthly consumption figures during the pilot so he can include them in the IT budget for 2025.

## 6. Content filter

Jeroen reported that the request for an adjusted content filter configuration has been submitted. Sarah asked that the reason for the adjustment is documented in the DPIA file. Pieter will provide a short note.

## Decisions

- D1: Evaluation with 12 physicians, baseline then drafts, start 2024-06-24.
- D2: Repeat pseudonymisation test before the evaluation starts.
- D3: Monthly consumption reporting from the start of the pilot.
