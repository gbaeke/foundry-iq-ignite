## 1. Approach

Terminal staff do not sit in classrooms. Training therefore has to fit into existing moments: the shift briefing, the onboarding day for new staff, and the shift leaders' monthly meeting. The core of the approach is a **copilot champion per shift**, building on the two pilot shift leaders who already show the bot to new colleagues.

Materials are prepared in Dutch and French, with an English version of the quick reference card. Everything must work without a projector, on a phone or printed.

## 3. Champion session (90 minutes)

1. **What the copilot is and is not** (15 min). It answers from SOPs and work instructions in SharePoint. It does not know live operations, the vessel plan or straddle carrier availability.
2. **Live demo with real questions** (20 min). Reefer alarm, damaged seal, gate exception, lashing inspection. In Dutch and French.
3. **Why you might not get an answer** (15 min). Permission trimming explained simply: "the copilot only uses documents you can open yourself". Two colleagues can get different answers to the same question, and that is correct. This was the most confusing point in the pilot.
4. **Feedback and reporting** (15 min). How to use thumbs down with a reason; how to report an outdated SOP to the content owner of the site.
5. **Briefing script practice** (20 min). Champions practise the 10-minute briefing with each other.
6. **Questions** (5 min).

## 4. Ten-minute shift briefing (script)

- Open the app in Teams on your phone or PC (30 s)
- Ask one question together, out loud (2 min)
- Show the source link and the version date (1 min)
- Show "Ask the shift leader" (1 min)
- Explain thumbs up/down (1 min)
- Hand out the quick reference card (30 s)
- Everyone asks one question of their own (4 min)

## 5. Quick reference card (one page, A5)

- Three example questions per language
- "Ask short, specific questions. Mention the equipment or the zone."
- "Always check the source for safety-critical steps."
- "No answer? Ask your shift leader, then press thumbs down."
- Where to report a problem: champion of your shift

## 6. Open points

- Gate building PCs: training assumes personal sign-in, which Scaldis IT is introducing. If not ready, gate clerks use phones.
- Night shift briefing: champions of the night shift get the session separately, on 2 September at 21:30.
- Measuring adoption: weekly active users per shift in the quality report, shared with the champions.
