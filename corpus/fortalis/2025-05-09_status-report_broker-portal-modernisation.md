## 2. Progress this period

- **Internal interviews.** All five done in the week of 22 April: two broker account managers, one underwriter, two claims handlers. Clear message from the claims handlers: a large part of their phone calls come from brokers asking for claim status.
- **Broker interviews.** 10 of 14 done, in Dutch and French, at the broker offices or by video call. The remaining four are planned for 12 to 16 May.
- **Observations.** 4 of 6 done. Elien Jacobs spent a Monday morning at a mid-sized broker office after a weekend of heavy rain. The office handled 23 claim calls that morning; the portal was opened eleven times, mostly to look up policy details, not to report claims.
- **Helpdesk tickets.** Export received from Laura Vermeulen on 2025-04-24 and fully categorised: 400 tickets over twelve months. First count: **41% are questions about the status of a claim**, by far the largest category.
- **Usage logs.** A home insurance quote takes 9 screens. More than half of the started quotes are abandoned before the end.

## 3. Early themes (not yet validated)

1. Brokers cannot see where a claim stands and call instead.
2. Double entry between the broker's own office software and the portal.
3. The quote flow is too long for simple products.
4. Brokers want to report claims from a phone, on site with the customer.

These will be tested in the co-creation workshops on 13 and 21 May.
