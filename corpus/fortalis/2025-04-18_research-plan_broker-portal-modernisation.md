# Research Plan and Interview Guide: Broker Portal discovery

| **Author** | Elien Jacobs, UX and Frontend Developer (UX lead), Kestrel Consulting |

## 1. Research questions

1. Which tasks do brokers do in the portal, how often, and which do they avoid?
2. Where do brokers lose time, and what do they do instead (phone, e-mail, retyping)?
3. What information do brokers need that they cannot find?
4. How do internal teams (account managers, underwriters, claims handlers) experience the portal from their side?
5. Which improvements would change a broker's working day the most?

## 2. Participants

**Brokers (target 14).** Selected from the 25 offices proposed by the broker account management team:

| Criterion | Spread |
|---|---|
| Office size | one-person office up to about 30 staff |
| Language | about half Dutch, half French speaking; at least one in the German-speaking region if possible |
| Region | Flanders, Brussels, Wallonia |
| Product mix | mainly private lines, mixed, mainly small business |
| Portal use | heavy users and offices that mostly e-mail their account manager |

**Observations (target 6).** A subset of the interviewed offices, half a day each, preferably on a Monday or a day after a storm, when claims come in.

**Internal (5).** Two broker account managers, one underwriter, two claims handlers.

## 3. Interview guide (brokers, 60 minutes)

**Warm-up (5 min).** Your office, your customers, your role.

**A typical week (10 min).** Walk me through last week. When did you open the portal? What for?

**Recent tasks (20 min).** Show me the last claim you reported. The last quote you made. What happened next? Where did you get stuck?

**Workarounds (10 min).** When do you call or e-mail Fortalis instead of using the portal? What do you keep in your own office software? What do you retype?

**Information needs (10 min).** What do you wish you could see about your customers' claims and policies? What figures do you trust, and which not?

**Closing (5 min).** If you could change one thing tomorrow, what would it be?

Rules for the interviewer: ask for examples, not opinions. Ask to see the screen. Do not mention ideas for a new portal. Do not promise anything.

## 4. Observation guide

- Note every switch between the portal and other tools (paper, phone, office software, e-mail).
- Time the main tasks: claim report, quote, document search.
- Note who in the office uses the portal and who avoids it.
- Photograph sticky notes, cheat sheets and printed screens, with permission, without customer data.

## 5. Helpdesk tickets and usage logs

- Ticket export from the last twelve months, broker identity removed. Categorise every ticket by topic and by portal section; target about 400 tickets.
- Usage logs: sessions per day, abandoned flows, time per screen in the quote flow.

## 6. Personal data and consent

- Information sheet and consent form in Dutch and French, signed before each interview.
- Recording only with consent; recordings stored in the Fortalis project space, never on personal devices.
- Notes use participant codes (B01 to B14, I01 to I05). The key between code and name is kept by Elien only.
- Recordings deleted at the end of the discovery; pseudonymised notes kept for the follow-up phase, if any.
- Brokers may withdraw at any time; their notes are then deleted.

## 7. Analysis

Affinity mapping of findings per research question, then a frequency count (how many brokers mention or show the problem) and an impact estimate together with Laura. Findings appear in the report only if seen in at least three offices, or in the ticket data.
