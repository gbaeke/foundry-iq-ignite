## 1. Context

On 2025-06-20 Marc Dubois informed Kestrel that the Broker Portal Modernisation project is cancelled after the discovery phase. The budget for the portal was reallocated to Fortalis' DORA compliance programme. There is no follow-up phase planned. This note records how the discovery material was closed and archived, as asked by Lotte Willems in her memo of the same day.

## 3. Personal data of participants

We promised brokers and internal participants in the information sheet that recordings would be deleted at the end of the discovery and that notes would be pseudonymised. Actions taken:

- **Recordings.** All 19 interview and observation recordings were deleted from the project space on 2025-06-25. Deletion confirmed with Laura, who checked the folder herself. The two brokers who refused recording have none.
- **Code key.** The list linking participant codes (B01 to B14, I01 to I05) to names was destroyed on 2025-06-25. The notes can no longer be traced to a person by Kestrel or Fortalis.
- **Photos.** Photos taken during observations were checked again: three still showed part of a customer name on a screen or paper. These were cropped or deleted.
- **Contact details.** Broker e-mail addresses used for invitations were removed from the Kestrel side. Fortalis keeps them in its normal broker relationship records.
- **Kestrel devices.** No research material was stored on Kestrel laptops. I confirmed this for my own laptop; Thomas confirmed for his.

## 4. Message to participants

On 2025-06-24 the broker account management team sent a short thank-you message to the 14 broker offices, in Dutch or French, with a one-page summary of the findings. The message says that Fortalis will use the results in its future plans for the portal, without announcing a date. Laura reviewed the wording so that it creates no expectations.

## 5. Budget

The discovery stayed within the time-and-materials cap of EUR 30,000. Thomas sends the final timesheet overview and the last invoice today.

## 6. Personal reflection

The work itself went well: brokers were open, the claims handlers and account managers gave us their time, and the steering committee recognised the findings. The roadmap is concrete enough to start from without redoing the research, as long as it is picked up within a year or so. After that, broker habits and the internal processes may have moved on and a short refresh of the research would be wise.

Things I would keep for a next discovery:

- Observations on a Monday after bad weather showed more than any interview.
- Repeating "this is not a promise" at the start of every session kept expectations under control.
- Deleting recordings at the end, as promised, takes half a day. Plan it.

*Elien Jacobs*
