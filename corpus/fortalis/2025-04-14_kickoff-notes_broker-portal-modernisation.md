## 1. Context

Marc explained why Fortalis starts with a discovery instead of a build. The broker portal is more than ten years old. Brokers complain about it in every account review, and the broker account managers spend a lot of time on questions the portal should answer. Several vendors have already pitched replacement products to Fortalis. Marc does not want to choose a solution before knowing what brokers actually need: "First understand, then decide."

Laura is product owner because most of the broker complaints she hears are about claims: brokers report claims through the portal and then lose sight of them. She will work closely with the broker account management team for everything outside claims (quotes, new policies, portfolio).

## 2. Scope of the discovery

- Understand how brokers use the portal today, where it fails them and what they work around.
- Understand the internal side: account managers, underwriters, claims handlers.
- Produce findings and a prioritised roadmap for a modernised portal.
- **Not in scope:** choosing a technology, platform or vendor, and building anything beyond paper or clickable sketches for the workshops.

Contract: time and materials, capped at **EUR 30,000**. Period: from today until the steering committee decision, expected in June.

Elien stressed that observations matter more than interviews: "Brokers will tell us the portal is slow. Watching them, we'll see what they do instead."

## 4. Discussion

- **Recruiting brokers.** The account management head will propose 25 broker offices; Elien selects for variety (size, region, language, product mix). Participation is voluntary; brokers get no compensation but will receive a summary of the findings.
- **Personal data.** Interviews are recorded only with consent, notes are pseudonymised, and recordings are deleted at the end of the discovery. Elien will prepare a short information sheet and consent form in Dutch and French.
- **Helpdesk tickets.** Laura will ask for an export of the last twelve months, without broker contact details.
- **Expectations.** Marc asked to avoid creating expectations with brokers: the discovery does not mean a new portal is coming this year. The information sheet will say so.
- **Architecture.** Kevin Hermans, Enterprise Architect, could not attend. Thomas will brief him; any technology question goes to him later.
