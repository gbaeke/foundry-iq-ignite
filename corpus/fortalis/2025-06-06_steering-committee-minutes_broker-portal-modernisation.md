## 1. Status of the discovery

Thomas reported that all planned activities are complete: 14 broker interviews, 6 contextual observations at broker offices, 5 internal interviews, analysis of 400 helpdesk tickets and the usage logs, and two co-creation workshops (13 and 21 May). The discovery report was shared as a draft on 2025-05-30. Budget: about 88% of the EUR 30,000 cap used; the remainder covers the report finalisation and the follow-up of today's decisions.

## 2. Findings (Elien)

Elien presented the six key findings of the report:

1. Claims follow-up is the biggest pain: 41% of helpdesk tickets are "where is my claim?" questions.
2. Double entry between the brokers' own office software and the portal.
3. The quote flow is too long for simple products: 9 screens for a home insurance quote.
4. Documents are scattered across sections with different search behaviour.
5. Mobile use is real: brokers want to report a claim with photos on site.
6. Trust in data is low because portfolio figures are refreshed overnight.

She showed two video fragments from the observations (with consent) and the paper prototype of the claim status page from workshop 1. The head of broker account management said the findings match what his team hears every week, "but now with numbers".

Marc asked whether any finding surprised the team. Elien: the extent of the workarounds. One office keeps a spreadsheet of all open claims and calls Fortalis every two weeks for each of them.

## 3. Proposed roadmap

| Step | Focus | Indicative timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Claim status tracking for brokers, with notifications | first 4 months |
| 2 | Mobile claim reporting with photo upload | months 3 to 6 |
| 3 | Simplified quote flow for home and motor | months 5 to 9 |
| 4 | Unified document centre | months 8 to 12 |
| 5 | Integration with broker office software | to be investigated |

Laura supports starting with step 1. It addresses the largest share of complaints, and the claim steps already exist in the claims process; she confirmed on 2025-05-20 that five of the six steps brokers asked for are recorded today.

Discussion on the build-or-buy question: several vendors have presented portal products to Fortalis. The committee agreed that this cannot be decided on the basis of user research alone and needs an architecture view.

## 4. Next phase (with Kevin Hermans)

Kestrel proposed as the first activity of a next phase a short architecture assessment with the Fortalis enterprise architecture team: how claim status data can be exposed to brokers, integration options with the claims process, identity of broker users, and a build-or-buy comparison. Kevin agreed that this is the right order and asked that the assessment does not assume any product.

Thomas presented a first indication of the next phase: architecture assessment (about four weeks), followed by design and build of roadmap step 1. A formal proposal will follow if the committee wants to continue.

## 5. Budget and decision

Marc explained that the board reviews all budgets for the second half of the year in its meeting of 2025-06-19, and that several regulatory programmes are competing for the same budget this year. He cannot commit to a next phase today.

**Decision:** the decision on the next phase and its budget is taken by Marc by **2025-06-20**, after the board meeting.
