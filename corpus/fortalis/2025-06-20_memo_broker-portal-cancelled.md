Hi all,

Short update on Fortalis, so nobody hears it in the corridor first.

Marc Dubois, Chief Digital Officer at Fortalis Verzekeringen, called me this morning: the **Broker Portal Modernisation** project is **cancelled** after the discovery phase, effective today. There will be no follow-up phase this year.

The reason has nothing to do with the quality of the work. Fortalis has to deliver on its **DORA compliance programme** (the EU Digital Operational Resilience Act for financial entities), and the board decided to **reallocate the budget** that was reserved for the portal to DORA. Marc was quite open about it: regulatory deadlines win, the portal waits. He did say the discovery report is "exactly what they needed" and that it will be the starting point if the portal comes back on the agenda.

Practical consequences:

- The discovery phase is closed. Thomas (Maes), please send the final timesheet overview and the last invoice for the discovery work by next Friday.
- Elien (Jacobs), please archive the interview notes and workshop material in the Fortalis project space, and make sure personal data of the brokers we interviewed is handled according to what we promised them.
- The time we had pencilled in for July is free again. I'll come back with planning changes next week.

One more thing worth knowing: **Kevin Hermans**, Enterprise Architect at Fortalis, is **leaving Fortalis at the end of June 2025**. Kevin has been our main architecture counterpart there and a pleasant one to work with. Marc hasn't named a successor yet. If you have anything open with Kevin, close it or hand it over before he goes, and let's send him a proper "merci" note.

On DORA itself: I've told Marc we're happy to talk if they need help on the cloud and resilience side, but I don't expect anything soon. No action needed from you on that.

Thanks, and bon weekend,

Lotte
